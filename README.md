# Foxyy Bruh

Someone says `bruh`. You say `bruh`.

Foxyy Bruh is a little chat addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever** that joins
in when someone says a word you've picked. It starts with `bruh`, but you can
add your own words and choose where you want it to reply.

## Make it your own

Type **`/bruh`** to open the settings.

- **Pick your words.** Add a word, switch it on or off, or remove it altogether.
  Yes, you can remove `bruh` too.
- **Pick your chats.** Choose guild, group and nearby chat, whispers, or the
  public and custom channels you've joined.
- **Keep your preferences.** Each character has its own settings, and updating
  the addon won't reset them.

To add a word, enter it under **Words to echo** and click **Add word** or press
Enter. The switches let you pause individual words without deleting them.

It matches a word on its own, not part of a sentence. With `bruh` enabled,
`BRUH` gets a lowercase `bruh` reply, but `hey bruh` doesn't.

**Public and custom channels start switched off.** To use one, turn on
**Public / custom channels** and then enable the channel under **Joined channels**.
The other supported chats start enabled.

## Installation

1. Click **Code > Download ZIP** on this page and extract the download.
2. Rename the extracted folder to `foxyy_bruh` and put it in your Forever
   installation's `Interface/AddOns` folder. Don't put it inside another addon folder.
3. Fully restart WoW, enable **Foxyy Bruh** in the AddOns list, and type `/bruh`.

Updating? Replace the files in the existing `foxyy_bruh` folder, then use `/reload`.

## Quick commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/bruh` | Open settings |
| `/bruh on` | Turn the addon on |
| `/bruh off` | Turn the addon off |
| `/bruh status` | Check whether it's on |

## A few things to know

- Your own messages won't trigger a reply. There's also a **3-second cooldown
  per chat** to keep things from getting too noisy.
- WoW doesn't allow automatic replies everywhere. In public/custom channels and
  outdoor say/yell, you'll get a **Send reply** button instead. Click it within
  three seconds if you want to join in.
- Everyone who wants to reply needs the addon. The person starting the joke doesn't.
- Battle.net whispers and communities aren't included.
- The cooldown helps, but several people using the addon can still start a chain
  of replies. If it gets annoying, switch off that chat or use `/bruh off`.

This is an early version for WoW Forever and still needs in-game testing.
